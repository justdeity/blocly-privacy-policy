Blocly Privacy Policy

Version: 21 September 2026

1. Developer

Blocly is developed by JustCompany. Contact: ravenxd@bk.ru.

2. Data

The game stores progress, current game, scores, records, settings, achievements, language, nickname and consent locally. A guest game identifier and nickname are used for rankings and rewards. No password or mandatory registration is required.

3. Advertising

Blocly uses the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK. The SDK may process IP address, approximate region, advertising and technical identifiers, device model, OS, app version, impressions and ad interactions. Personalized advertising is enabled only by a separate user choice.

Provider policies: Yandex and AppMetrica.

4. Choices and deletion

The policy must be accepted on first launch. Settings let you disable personalized ads, change nickname and open “Delete data”. After confirmation, local progress, records, settings, achievements, saves, nickname and identifier are permanently deleted. This works offline.

5. Storage and rights

Local data is kept until app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Contact ravenxd@bk.ru to request access, correction or deletion. Do not send passwords or payment details.

6. Children and changes

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Material changes update this policy and show it again.
